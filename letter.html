CHAPTER 03 · SOMETHING FROM MY HEART

a few words,
just for you.

Dear, my favorite person,

I don't think I say it enough, but I'm really glad that you're in my life. You make ordinary days feel extraordinary in a way I couldn't even describe with words, and even the smallest things become memories I want to keep forever.

I hope you know that you don't have to be perfect or have everything figured out to be loved. I love you, I love you as a person, as a partner, as a friend, I love when you are you. Its the way you notice whenever I need any help, the way you laugh, and all the moments that probably seem small to you but mean a lot to me.

Whatever life brings us, I hope we keep choosing kindness, keep learning each other, and keep finding reasons to be together. Thank you for being here, and for being someone I get to care about this much.

with all my heart, always. <3
